# EXPERTISE.md — Domain Knowledge Base

## Pakistan Market Specifics
- **Units:** Marla (25 sq.yd ≈ 225 sq.ft), Kanal (20 Marla), Sq.Yd, Sq.Ft
- **Cities:** Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Gujranwala, Peshawar, Quetta
- **Popular societies:** DHA, Bahria Town, Gulberg, Johar Town, Model Town, Capital Smart City
- **Vehicle brands:** Toyota, Honda, Suzuki, Kia, Hyundai, MG, United, Road Prince
- **Mobile brands:** Apple, Samsung, Infinix, Tecno, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Itel
- **PTA:** Pakistan Telecommunication Authority — non-approved phones lose ~30% resale value
- **Payment rails:** Raast (SBP instant), JazzCash, Easypaisa, 1Link
- **Regulators:** SECP (companies), FBR (tax), PTA (telecom), SBP (payments), CCP (competition)
- **Laws:** PECA 2016, Finance Act 2025, E-commerce Policy, Import Policy Order

## Industry Terms
- **Marla / Kanal** — property area units
- **Fard** — land ownership document
- **NOC** — No Objection Certificate (development authority)
- **Registry** — legal property transfer document
- **STRN** — Sales Tax Registration Number
- **NTN** — National Tax Number
- **CNIC** — Computerised National Identity Card
- **PTA-approved** — phone legally registered with PTA
- **COD** — Cash on Delivery
- **Raast** — State Bank instant payment system

## Best Practices (Researched)
- Multi-step forms: 7–8 fields per step optimal; completion 40% → 85%
- Show progress bar always
- Inline validation
- Save & resume across devices
- Camera-first upload on mobile
- On-device image compression (data cost)
- WhatsApp deep-links for contact
- Urdu RTL support
- PWA fallback for low-end devices
