# MARKET_RESEARCH.md

## Market Size
- Pakistan classifieds & portals market CAGR: **7.2% (2019–2030)**
- Drivers: internet penetration, mobile commerce, urbanisation
- Constraints: intense competition, trust deficits, regulatory complexity

## Key Findings
1. **OLX has divested from Pakistan** — refocusing on 7 core markets. Structural opportunity.
2. **COD = ~90% of e-commerce** in Pakistan; escrow is under-penetrated.
3. **No incumbent offers escrow + verification + logistics** end-to-end.
4. **WhatsApp is de facto commerce layer** — users share numbers and move off-platform.
5. **Tier-2/3 cities** are the largest untapped growth (Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar).
6. **Hyperlocal segmentation** is the backbone of classifieds defensibility.
7. **Mobile-first, camera-first, data-sensitive** users.
8. **PTA approval** on phones is non-negotiable for resale value.
9. **Marla/Kanal** are the only meaningful property units in Pakistan.
10. **FBR Finance Act 2025** mandates STRN for online sellers — compliance is a moat.

## Trust Gap (Primary Opportunity)
- Advance payment requests
- Off-platform move requests
- Unreasonably low prices
- Mismatched photos
- Fake job postings / MLM schemes
- Delayed payouts (OLX reportedly >30 days vs 15-day promise)
