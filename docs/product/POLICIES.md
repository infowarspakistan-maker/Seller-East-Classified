## Mandatory Content Snippets
- **Terms:** business name, registered address, pricing, listing rules, prohibited items, limitation of liability, Pakistan governing law, city jurisdiction.
- **Privacy:** data collected (CNIC, phone, location), usage, third-party sharing (PSPs, couriers), retention, user rights.
- **Prohibited Items (v1):** weapons, drugs, counterfeit goods, adult content, stolen goods, items banned under Import Policy Order.
- **Seller Code:** genuineness warranty, counterfeit policy, blacklist criteria.

## Compliance
- **Finance Act 2025:** STRN mandatory for all sellers; monthly FBR reporting.
- **PECA 2016:** intermediary liability; 24-hour takedown on notice.
- **E-commerce Policy:** enforceable seller contracts.
