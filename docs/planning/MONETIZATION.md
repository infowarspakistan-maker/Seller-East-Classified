# MONETIZATION.md

| Stream | Model | Phase | Pricing (PKR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Promoted listings | Flat fee top-of-search | 1 | 500–2,000/week |
| Escrow fee | 1–2% of txn value | 1 | split buyer/seller |
| Verification fast-track | Priority CNIC/NTN | 1 | 200–500 |
| Inspection service | Revenue share | 2 | 2,000–5,000 |
| Store subscriptions | Monthly | 2 | 5,000–15,000 |
| Lead generation | Per-lead/package | 2 | market |
| Display ads | CPM | 3 | market |
| Logistics margin | Markup | 3 | thin |

## Philosophy
- Never charge buyers to browse or contact.
- Charge for **visibility, trust, convenience**.
- Delay monetisation until liquidity — promoted listings have no value before buyer traffic.
